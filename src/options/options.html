ScrollPeak

A scrollbar minimap for Firefox, ported from Kate. The scrollbar settings are Kate's, mapped one for one; the preview's size is an addition, because Kate hard-codes his at half the window by a fifth.

Scrollbar

Show the scrollbar

Kate's default is 60px, range 0–999. It is capped at 200 here because beyond that it eats the page.

Preview

Both are a percentage of the window. Kate draws the preview at half the width and a fifth of the height, which is where they start.

    Appearance

    By default the strip is a darker shade of your Firefox theme's background, or the page's if no theme is installed, and the marks are nudged until they contrast with it. The marks are the page's own text colours, because a page chose them against the page, not against our strip.

    A WCAG contrast ratio, 1 to 21. 3 is enough for a one-pixel mark that is carrying shape rather than reading as text. The page's own colours are kept as-is once they clear the bar; only their lightness moves, so a link stays link-coloured.
